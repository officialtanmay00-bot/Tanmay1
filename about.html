The Craft Behind Every Frame

I'm Tanmay Gupta — a driven VFX student currently studying in Noida, originally from Bhiwani, Haryana. My journey into visual storytelling began with a deep fascination for how film and motion can evoke raw emotion.

Today I channel that fascination into 4K video editing, compositing, motion graphics, and graphic design. Every project I take on is treated with the same level of dedication — whether it's a short commercial reel or a full brand identity.

I believe creativity without discipline is wasted potential. That's why every deadline I commit to is a promise I keep — always.
